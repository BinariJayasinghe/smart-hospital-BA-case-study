# Smart Hospital Management System – BA Analysis Case Study 

## Overview

This project demonstrates the application of Business Analysis techniques to evaluate and improve hospital operations through a Smart Hospital Management System.

The objective is to identify operational challenges, analyze root causes, prioritize requirements, and recommend digital transformation initiatives.

---

## BA Techniques Applied

- SWOT Analysis
- GAP Analysis
- Root Cause Analysis
- 5 Whys Analysis
- Fishbone Diagram
- BPMN Modeling
- Process Mapping (AS-IS & TO-BE)
- MoSCoW Prioritization

---

## Business Problem

The hospital currently experiences:

- Long patient waiting times
- Manual appointment scheduling
- Paper-based patient records
- Delayed billing processes
- Poor departmental communication
- Limited inventory visibility

---

## Proposed Solution

Implementation of a centralized Smart Hospital Management System featuring:

- Online Appointment Booking
- Electronic Health Records (EHR)
- Automated Billing
- Real-Time Inventory Tracking
- Digital Prescriptions
- Queue Management

---

## Expected Benefits

- Reduced waiting time
- Improved patient experience
- Better operational efficiency
- Faster billing process
- Increased data accuracy
- Improved inventory management
